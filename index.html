About My Research

I am an astrophysicist specializing in cosmology, with a focus on understanding the large‑scale structure of the universe, the nature of dark energy, and the evolution of cosmic structures over time.

My work combines theoretical modeling, numerical simulations, and observational data analysis to tackle fundamental questions about our universe's composition, evolution, and ultimate fate.

Research Interests:

  • Large‑Scale Structure & Cosmic Web
  • Dark Energy & Modified Gravity
  • Cosmic Microwave Background
  • Galaxy Formation & Evolution
  • Numerical Methods & High‑Performance Computing
  • Machine Learning in Astrophysics
