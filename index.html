About My Research

I am an MSc Astrophysics student at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU), working in cosmology, early universe physics, and transient astrophysics.

My research focuses on gamma-ray bursts, supernovae, and other transient events, combining theoretical models, numerical methods, and observational data.

Research Experience

LMU Munich & Wendelstein Observatory

2025 – Present

Photometry, image calibration, transient analysis, and follow-up observations of gamma-ray bursts and supernovae. Contributing to NASA GCN Circulars.

Cosmology Research (Sharif University of Technology)

2020 – 2024

Studied inflation, early universe cosmology, and primordial black holes using analytical and numerical methods.

Life Beyond Research

I have been obsessed with astrophysics and science since I was a kid. I always wanted to become a scientist, like the ones I saw in cartoons such as Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs.

Before university, a big part of my life was music and sports.

I played violin as a child, later learned viola, and performed in orchestras for several years. I still play sometimes for myself.

I also started skating as a kid, then moved into inline hockey and ice hockey. I played as a defense player, competed in national leagues, and won two bronze medals.

Later I became a skating and hockey coach, and some of my students went on to international competitions.

After a serious injury, I had to stop competitive sports, which changed my direction and brought me closer to physics and research.

Right now, I like learning multiple languages at the same time, and I also enjoy hiking with my dog.

My Dog

My dog came into my life when I needed him the most. Since then, he has been with me through every stage of my life.

We went through everything together — from university entrance exams in Iran, to getting accepted into one of the best universities, and through my bachelor’s degree.

He even moved with me to Germany so I could continue my master’s degree.

He is my biggest academic partner. He doesn’t understand physics or cosmology, but he has been there through every step of it.

He is my biggest motivation, and I love him very much. I want to make him proud one day, so that when he meets other dogs at the park and they are all sniffing around, he can somehow tell them that my mama is a scientist.
